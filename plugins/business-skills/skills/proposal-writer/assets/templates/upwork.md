## Hook

One or two sentences. Name the result they asked for and any boundary from the fit score.

## Understanding

Restate the work, the rate or fixed price if it was stated, and the constraint you will not ignore.

## Approach

Three or four sentences. The first milestone only. Say what you will hand back and what you will not start.

## Proof

- Copy a profile proof bullet exactly.
- Copy another if it is actually relevant. Otherwise stop at one.

## Timeline

The first deliverable, and what has to be true before a second milestone.

## Questions

- A question that changes the estimate.
- A question that shows you read a specific line in the post.
