## Hook

One sentence on what the custom offer includes.

## Understanding

The buyer's message, restated as a scope. Quote the part you are refusing if they asked for unpaid discovery.

## Approach

In: the files you will deliver.
Out: the work you will not do inside this price (revisions beyond the offer, rendering a finished video, logging into their accounts).

## Proof

- Exact profile proof bullets only.

## Timeline

The first file they receive, and what you need from them before the clock starts.

## Questions

- What does a finished version of this look like in their hands?
- Which input file should the sample be built from?
