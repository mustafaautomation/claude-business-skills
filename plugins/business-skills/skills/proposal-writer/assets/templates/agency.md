## Hook

The outcome, who it is for, and the decision you need from them.

## Understanding

The request in their words, plus the risk you see in the way it was scoped.

## Approach

Name two phases at most. Phase one is something they can reject without paying for phase two. Mention what is deliberately left out.

## Proof

- Exact profile proof bullets only.

## Timeline

The first deliverable and the review point. Do not draw a full Gantt from a one-paragraph brief.

## Questions

- Who signs off on the first deliverable?
- Which constraint in the brief is fixed, and which one is a preference?
