Title: One argument, four channels

You already recorded the long version. A client call, a teardown, a talk. The mistake is pasting that transcript into LinkedIn and calling it a post.

A transcript is one argument buried under every time you repeated yourself. Pull the argument out. Say it in a sentence a stranger can use. Then give each channel a different job.

LinkedIn gets the argument, one concrete moment, and a reason to comment. Not a recap of your career.

The X thread gets the same argument in pieces small enough to read on a phone. The first post is the claim. The last post tells people what to do next. Do not open with "1/".

The newsletter is where the teaching lives. Three subject lines, a preview that stands alone, and at least two sections. People asked for this one. Respect that.

The short video is 20 to 45 seconds. Hook in the first line. Three beats you can say out loud. On-screen text that matches the spoken line. A close that names the next step.

If a draft fails a length check, fix the draft. Do not ship it and hope the algorithm is in a good mood.
