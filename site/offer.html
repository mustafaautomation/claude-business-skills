FAQ

Who owns the files?

You do. Upload them, edit them, keep them in your repo.

Do you send the email or post the draft?

No. The skill drafts. You send.

Does the invoice skill know if the tax is legal?

No. It checks that the arithmetic agrees. The rate for a jurisdiction is an accountant’s question.

Does the video skill export a finished cut?

No. It writes a cut list and a CMX EDL from a timestamped transcript. If there are no timestamps, it asks for them instead of inventing any.

Will CI call a paid model API?

Not unless you ask and you provide the key. The public scripts do not.

No. Portfolio demo, not client work. The numbers on the sample invoices are fictional.
